What to have ready before you get in touch

Giving support everything they need in one go makes the fix much faster. You'll usually need:

  • Your username (never your password)
  • The date and rough time of the problem
  • For a transaction — the amount, the method and the transaction ID
  • A screenshot of any message shown on screen
  • The problem, explained clearly in one line

What support will never ask for

Never give these three to anyone

Your password — real support doesn't need it. Your wallet PIN — no platform asks for your bKash, Nagad or Rocket PIN. An OTP — the verification code sent to your phone is for you alone. If anyone asks for these, whoever they claim to be, it's a scam.

What to expect

It helps to have realistic expectations once you've contacted support. General questions get solved quickly on live chat. But anything that needs checks or an internal review — a stuck transaction or a verification, for example — takes time, and that's normal.

Sending a fresh message about the same problem again and again doesn't speed things up; each new message can join the queue as a separate request. Stay in one conversation and quote your reference number if you have one.

If it isn't resolved: keep everything you have together — dates, amounts, transaction IDs and screenshots of earlier conversations. If a dispute drags on, those records are what help most. Language

The official platform's interface is in English. Whether support is available in Bengali couldn't be verified independently, so this page makes no promise about it.

If writing in English feels awkward, describe the problem in short, simple sentences and add a screenshot if you can — a picture bridges much of the language gap. Writing dates, amounts and transaction IDs as numbers leaves less room for misunderstanding.
