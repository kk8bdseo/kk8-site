Turnover — the condition that matters most

Before taking a bonus, look at the turnover requirement, not the percentage. Turnover is how much you have to bet before you can withdraw the money, bonus included. High-percentage offers often carry higher turnover, so an offer that looks big can be worth less in practice.

A simple rule: if you only want to play with the money you deposit, skipping the bonus is the straightforward path — there's no turnover requirement, and you can withdraw at any time. Bonuses are optional, never compulsory.

Fake bonus links — a warning

The promise of a bonus is the most common bait in scams. In Bangladesh, "free credit links" and "special bonus codes" are spread on social media and messaging apps, and they actually lead to fake login pages.

Spotting them is simple. A real offer always appears on the promotions page inside your own account — if it isn't there, it isn't an offer. You never need to log in through an outside link.

Be especially suspicious of: unusually big promises, rushing language ("only 2 hours left"), and being asked for a PIN or OTP up front. The detailed checklist is here — how to spot fake KK8 sites.

How useful an offer really is

A bonus is never extra money — it's borrowed play that comes with conditions. Once you see that difference, the decision gets much easier.

A bonus helps if you play regularly anyway and the turnover gets met naturally. But if you only play now and then for short spells, meeting the bonus conditions means playing more than you planned — and that is the real cost of the bonus.

Rebate offers are simpler in this respect, because they pay back a share of what you play and carry fewer complicated conditions. For new players, the safest route is to play without bonuses for the first few weeks and get to know the platform.

Rules for checking an offer

  • Always check an offer on the promotions page in your own account — not on the word of an outside site or message
  • Read the full terms, especially the turnover and the maximum withdrawal limit
  • Check how long the offer lasts
  • Don't trust any offer or link that claims a "guaranteed win" or "sure profit"
