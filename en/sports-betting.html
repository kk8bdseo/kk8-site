How a bet works — the basics

There are three basic ideas to understand before your first bet. They work the same way at every sportsbook.

Odds

Odds tell you two things — how much you could win, and how likely the bookmaker thinks the event is. The higher the odds, the bigger the possible return, but the less likely the event. Being drawn in by big odds is the most common mistake newcomers make.

Types of market

The simplest market is the match result — who will win. Beyond that, there's usually the handicap (giving one team an imaginary head start or deficit to even things up) and over/under (whether the total runs or goals will be above or below a set number). While you're new, it's best to stay with match-result markets — the risk in complex markets is harder to judge.

Live betting

Betting while a match is in progress is called live or in-play betting, and the odds change as the game goes on. It's fast and comparatively risky, because you have less time to decide. It's best not to bet live without a stable internet connection.
