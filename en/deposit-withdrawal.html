Keeping transactions safe

The money side is where the risk of fraud is highest, so always follow a few rules.

  • Never tell anyone your wallet PIN. Neither the platform nor support will ever ask for your bKash, Nagad or Rocket PIN. Anyone who asks is a scammer.
  • Don't share an OTP. The verification code sent to your phone is for you alone — hand it over and someone else can take control of your wallet.
  • Keep proof of each transaction. Save the transaction ID and time — if anything goes wrong, that's the most useful information you'll have.
  • Only use your own account. Paying from a family member's wallet causes a name mismatch when you withdraw.
  • Avoid transactions on public Wi-Fi. Use mobile data or your own Wi-Fi.

Keeping track of your transactions

Checking your account's transaction history regularly is a good habit. It does two things — anything odd gets caught quickly, and you see a real picture of how much you're spending.

Many players think they spend less than they do, because small deposits don't feel large one at a time. Looking at the monthly total makes the picture clear — and makes it easier to decide on a limit.
