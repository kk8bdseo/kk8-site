Before you start — eligibility and what to have ready

Check two things before you open an account. First, you must be {{minAge}} or older — this isn't a formality. If age verification turns up a mismatch, the account is closed and any money in it can be held.

Second, have these ready: an active mobile number registered in your own name, and a mobile wallet in the same name ({{paymentRails.0.name}}, {{paymentRails.1.name}} or {{paymentRails.2.name}}). A name mismatch is the number-one reason withdrawals get stuck later.

Registration steps

  1. Go to the official site. Type the address yourself or use the button on this page. Don't use a "register link" you found on social media.
  2. Click Register or Sign Up.
  3. Choose a username and password. Make the password at least eight characters, mixing letters and numbers. Don't reuse a password from any other site.
  4. Enter your real name — exactly as it appears on your national ID or bank records. No nicknames or shortened forms.
  5. Enter and verify your mobile number. Complete the check with the code sent to that number.
  6. Finish verification — now. Many people leave it for later and then get stuck when they try to withdraw. Doing it before your first deposit is the smart move.

What to know before you take a bonus

Bonus offers appear as soon as you've registered. Taking one isn't compulsory, and it isn't always worth it. Accepting a bonus usually adds a turnover requirement — you have to bet a set amount before you can withdraw the money, bonus included.

Don't decide on the size of the percentage; read the terms. If you only want to play with the money you deposit, skipping the bonus is the simplest route — you can then withdraw at any time. The promotions page explains the types of offer in detail.

Note that no offer specific to Bangladesh has been officially announced yet, so this site doesn't quote any amount. The promotions page inside your account shows the current details.

What verification is, and why it's needed

Many new players see verification as extra hassle, but it's actually there to protect you. It's how the platform confirms the account really belongs to you — so nobody else can withdraw money in your name.

For licensed operators it's also a regulatory requirement. KK8 operates under a {{licence.authority}} licence ({{licence.number}}), and identity checks are mandatory under that kind of licence. A platform that lets you withdraw with no checks at all is the one to be suspicious of.

What's usually requested: proof of identity (a national ID card or an equivalent document) and verification of your mobile number. When you photograph a document, keep all four corners in the frame, make sure the text is clearly readable, and don't let glare cover any part — unclear photos are the biggest reason documents have to be sent again.

One warning: only submit documents through the secure upload form inside the platform. If someone asks for your documents on WhatsApp, Telegram or email, it's almost certainly a scam — real support never asks for your ID in a chat or message.

After you register

Once your account is created, the next steps in order: log in to check the account opens properly, set up your deposit method, and if you play on your phone regularly, install the app.

Set a limit before you begin — write down how much you'll spend each week in advance. Trying to win back losses is the most common way losses grow.
