Turnover — the condition that matters most

Before claiming a bonus, check the turnover requirement, not just the bonus percentage. Turnover is the total amount you need to bet before the bonus conditions are completed and withdrawals become available. A larger bonus may also come with a higher turnover requirement.

If you prefer simpler conditions, you can choose not to claim a bonus and play using only your deposited funds. Bonuses are optional, so always check the terms before deciding.

Fake bonus links — a warning

Fake bonus links may lead players to unofficial login or registration pages, often using offers like "free credit" or "special bonus codes" to attract attention.

Always check promotions directly on the official KK8 platform after logging in. If a promotion is not listed there, avoid using external links.

Be cautious of unusually large bonus promotions, urgent messages, or requests for your PIN, OTP or password. See our How to Spot Fake KK8 Sites guide for more information.

How useful a promotion really is

A bonus is not the same as free cash. It usually comes with turnover requirements, eligible games and withdrawal rules, so check the terms before claiming.

Bonuses may suit regular players who can meet the requirements through normal play. For occasional players, the same requirements may encourage more play than planned.

Rebate promotions are generally simpler, returning a percentage based on eligible play. New players may prefer to understand the platform first before claiming more complex bonuses.

Rules for Checking a Promotion

  • Check promotions on the official promotions page or in your account, rather than relying on outside websites or messages.
  • Read the full terms, especially turnover requirements, eligible games and maximum withdrawal limits.
  • Check the start date, end date and claim period.
  • Avoid promotions or links promising "guaranteed wins" or "sure profit".
