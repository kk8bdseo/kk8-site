The games you'll usually find

Baccarat

The most popular game in Asian live casinos. The rule is simple — you bet on whether the Player or the Banker hand will finish closer to nine. It's quick to learn, which makes it popular with newcomers.

Roulette

The wheel spins and the ball stops on a number. You can bet on a single number, a colour, odd or even, and more. A single number pays the most, but it's also the least likely to come up.

Blackjack

You have to beat the dealer without going over 21. Your decisions matter more here than in other games — when you take a card and when you stop is what shapes the result.

How a round works

Each round at a live table follows a set rhythm, and knowing it in advance takes the awkwardness out of your first session.

  1. Once you're seated, betting stays open for a fixed time — a countdown shows on screen.
  2. When time runs out, betting closes and the dealer starts the round.
  3. The result is shown on screen, and winning bets are added to your balance automatically.
  4. Betting opens again for the next round.

Because of the countdown, your time to decide is limited — that's the biggest difference between live games and slots. On slots you play at your own pace; at a live table, the table sets the pace. So it's better not to sit down at a live game when you're tired or in a hurry.

Live casino or slots — which to choose

The two feel different, and which suits you depends on how you want to play.

Slots go at your own pace, use less data, last a long time on small stakes, and many have a demo mode. If you play in short bursts or you're on mobile data, slots are the better fit.

Live casino feels more personal — a real dealer, real cards, and in games like blackjack your own decisions genuinely count. But it needs a steady connection, uses more data, and you have to go at the table's pace.

Advice for newcomers

Live games move at a different speed from slots — there's a fixed time to decide each round, and that pressure pushes newcomers to rush. Keep your stakes small for your first few sessions and stick to one game.
