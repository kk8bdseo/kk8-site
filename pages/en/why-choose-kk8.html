The official site versus clones

Several websites active in Bangladesh use KK8's name without being official. Some copy the brand's name exactly; more often you'll see lookalike domains — one character changed, or an extra word added.

That creates two real risks. Money you deposit may never reach a real account, and your login details can end up with a third party.

  • Read the domain in the address bar letter by letter — don't rush it
  • Look for licence number {{licence.number}} in the footer
  • Don't log in through links in messages or on social media
  • Once you have the right address, bookmark it and always go in from there

Balance

This page keeps both the positives and the negatives, because a decision made from a list of benefits alone isn't a good decision. The verifiable side is strong — licence, providers, testing certification. The unverified points are mostly specific to Bangladesh: language, payment terms and offers.

Before you decide

No gaming platform is risk-free, and you shouldn't believe anyone who says otherwise. What you can do is decide on facts you can check — whether there's a licence, who the providers are, whether there are support channels.

And most important of all — set your own limit before you play. No platform, however good, can take that responsibility off you.
