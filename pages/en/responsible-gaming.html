kk8 responsible gaming — keeping play as entertainment. Setting limits, recognising the warning signs, and where to get support in Bangladesh.

The basic principles

  • Betting isn't a way to earn money. No strategy can guarantee a profit over the long run.
  • Only use money you can afford to lose. Never rent, school or medical money.
  • Don't borrow to play. Betting with borrowed money is the fastest way to lose.
  • Not under {{minAge}}. That's essential both legally and ethically.

Setting limits

The most effective step is to set limits before you play, not in the middle of it. Three kinds of limit work:

  1. A money limit — write down the most you'll spend in a week or a month, in advance.
  2. A time limit — how long one session lasts. Set an alarm.
  3. A loss limit — lose a set amount and you stop for the day. This is the most important one.

Warning signs

If you see any one of these in yourself, it's time to take a break:

  • You play again to win back what you lost
  • You hide how much you play from family or friends
  • You borrow money or sell things to play
  • It's affecting your work, studies or relationships
  • You feel restless or irritable when you're not playing
  • You keep breaking the limits you set yourself
