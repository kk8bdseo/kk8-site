How to log in to KK8

Logging in is simple, but each step carries a security point. In order:

  1. Type the address yourself. Enter the official address in your browser's address bar by hand, or use a bookmark you saved earlier. Clicking whatever link a search result shows you is the riskiest habit there is.
  2. Click the login button. It's usually at the top right of the page.
  3. Enter your username and password — exactly as you set them when you registered.
  4. Complete the security check if you're asked to.
  5. Check your account balance once you're in. If anything looks wrong, tell support straight away — the longer you wait, the harder it is to sort out.

If you play on your phone regularly, the KK8 app is more convenient: you never have to find the address in a browser again, which also means less chance of logging in to the wrong site.

If you forget your password

Use the "Forgot Password" option on the login page. A verification code is sent to the mobile number you registered with, and you use it to set a new password.

Follow three rules when you choose the new one: don't reuse a password from any other site; don't put your date of birth or mobile number in it; and never tell anyone your password — not even someone claiming to be from support. Real support staff never ask for your password.

How to recognise the real login page

Some sites active in Bangladesh use the KK8 name without being official. Their login pages look almost the same, but anything you type there goes to a third party. Before you log in, check these points:

  • Read the domain letter by letter. Clone sites often change one character or add an extra word.
  • Look for the licence number in the footer. The real platform shows {{licence.text}} {{licence.number}}.
  • Check for HTTPS — but remember, HTTPS doesn't mean a site is genuine. It's the minimum, not proof.
  • Ignore links sent in messages. Don't log in through a "bonus link" sent on WhatsApp, Telegram or Facebook.

App or browser — where to log in from

Most players in Bangladesh play on their phones, so this is a practical question. Both options have their own advantages.

Logging in through the app is usually quicker: there's no address to type, and your session stays active for longer. It's also safer — once the official app is installed, there's almost no chance of logging in to the wrong site, because the app connects to the right server by itself.

Logging in through a browser is handy when you don't want to install the app, or you're on a desktop. In that case, bookmark the address and always go in through that bookmark — searching for the link every time is the main way people end up on clone sites.

Whichever you use, one rule always applies — don't log in on a shared or public device. If you have to use a cyber café computer or someone else's phone, always log out when you finish, and choose "No" if the browser offers to save your password.
